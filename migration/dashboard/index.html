Mission Principles

The discipline behind every trade

These institutional axioms are permanently enshrined in the Rulebook to maintain 100% operational focus on the Home Dashboard.

I

Longevity First

Survive long enough for compounding to work. Never break risk parameters or chase desperate trades. Capital defense is the only absolute priority.

II

Process Over Result

Execution discipline matters infinitely more than short-term P&L. A winning trade executed off-plan is a failure; a disciplined loss executed on-plan is an asset.

III

Detach From Single Trades

Don't get emotionally tied to one outcome. Think exclusively in 20-trade statistical sets. Individual trades are just coins tossed by your positive expectancy.

IV

Focus on Horizons

Review weekly at minimum, ideally monthly. Edges and psychological patterns only reveal their true nature across time and institutional sample sizes.

Core Principles

  • Protect Capital → Protect Psychology → Pursue Profits (Non-negotiable sequence).
  • Judge success by process before outcome: Grade trades on execution fidelity, not net ₹.
  • No trade is better than a bad trade: SOH (Sit On Hands) is a high-probability position.
  • Systems replace willpower: Emotion fluctuates; automation and circuit breakers endure.

Risk Parameters

  • Default Risk: 0.5%–1.0% maximum equity at stake per trade.
  • 3-Loss Breaker: Three consecutive losses triggers an immediate mandatory session shutdown.
  • Drawdown Defense: Strict daily max stop (-₹15k), weekly stop, and rolling equity milestone floors.
